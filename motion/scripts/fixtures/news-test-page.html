Fixture Model: a placeholder page for testing the news renderer

This page exists only to test the renderer. Its text is placeholder copy with no claims in it. It has headings to scroll between, a phrase to select, a table with a column to outline and a video to play.

Overview

The renderer pans this page between its headings. A cursor moves to whatever the narration points at, and the blue selection sweeps over a phrase the way a person drags across text in a browser.

Here is the phrase the test selects: this sentence is the highlighted phrase, and the text after it stays unselected. Longer phrases wrap onto a second line, so the sweep has to cross a line break and carry on from the left edge of the next line without a jump.
